Throughout the Years Campaign

Client

Challenge

To celebrate 40 years since it was first established, Argos wanted to mark this event by creating a series of videos to show how Argos was evolved throughout the years. This was to be used to promote the business external and show their colleagues how it’s transformed and adapted.

Solution

We created 41 animations systemically detailing the millstones Argos went through to be where it is today. The animations would act as an education piece for those viewing.

Outcome

Highly successful 40th marketing event – These animations were part of a massive PR & marketing exercise we were involved in and displayed on their intranet.
